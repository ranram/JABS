## Local media catalogs

The following directories are local media catalogs. Their media files are
intentionally excluded from Git; only their usage instructions are part of the
repository:

- `game-assets/*/characters/`
- `game-assets/*/portraits/`
- `players/`
- `sponsors/`
- `tourney-logos/`

screenshots, and similar media remain the property of their respective owners.
Users and distributors are responsible for obtaining permission to use and
redistribute the media they place in these catalogs. The Apache-2.0 license for

Testing references, screenshots, error captures, and research artifacts are
also excluded from the repository and application distributions.
